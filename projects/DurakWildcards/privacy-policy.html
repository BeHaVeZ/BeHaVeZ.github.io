Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Durak: Wildcards ("the game") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The game is made by AltForge Studio, which is responsible for your information under this policy ("we", "us").

Contact: support@altforgestudio.com

The short version

  • You can play offline without an account. Games against the computer need no sign-in, no network and no personal information.
  • Online play uses a game account run on Unity Gaming Services. It is created automatically and anonymously; you can link it to Google Play Games so your progress follows you to a new phone.
  • What other players can see is your player name and tag, your avatar, your friend code and — unless you turn on Hide my stats — your level, rating, results and recent matches.
  • Ads and analytics only run after you agree, and saying no never locks you out of the game.
  • You can delete your account from inside the game, or by emailing us.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information stored only on your device

The game saves your settings, your statistics and progress (wins, losses, level, rating), your coins and the cosmetic items you own, your last ten finished matches (as replays), an unfinished offline match so you can resume it, and a random identifier that tells your phone's saved statistics apart from another phone's. This identifier is generated by the game; it is not a hardware or advertising identifier. This information stays on your phone unless you use the online features described below.

1.2 Your game account (online features)

When you open the online parts of the game (online matches, friends, cloud backup, the store), the game signs you in to Unity Authentication and creates a player account. That account has:

  • a player ID assigned by Unity;
  • a player name you choose, with a number tag the service adds (for example bob#4821); if you link Google Play Games and have not chosen a name, your Play Games display name is used once as a starting point;
  • a friend code derived from your player ID.

1.3 Google Play Games (optional)

If you link Google Play Games, Google sends Unity Authentication a one-time sign-in code, and your account is linked to your Google Play Games player ID. The game reads your Play Games display name. We do not access your Google account's email address, contacts or files.

1.4 Cloud backup

While you are signed in, the game backs up your statistics, coins and owned items, and your recent match replays to Unity Cloud Save under your player ID, so they survive reinstalling or changing phones.

1.5 Your public profile

To let friends find you and see how you play, some of your information is published to other players of the game:

Always visible to other players Visible unless you turn on Hide my stats
Player name and tag, avatar, friend code Level, rating, win / loss totals, recent matches and their replays

Your name can be searched for by other players while Findable by name is on (the default). Turning it off removes you from name search; people can still add you with your friend code.

1.6 Online matches, lobbies and friends

  • Lobbies: when you host or join a table, your player name, tag, avatar, player ID, rating and — unless hidden — a short summary of your stats are shared with the other players at that table. With Hide my stats on, your rating is still used by the game to calculate rating changes, but it is not shown.
  • Matches: online matches are connected through Unity Relay servers; players never connect to each other directly, but the relay servers necessarily see your device's IP address. Your moves are sent to the table's host device. When a match ends, each player receives the full record of that match (every card and move, and the other players' names and player IDs) for their replay.
  • Friends: the Unity Friends service stores your friends list, friend requests, blocks and invites, and shows your friends whether you are online, when you were last seen, and the table you are hosting.
  • There is no chat in the game.

1.7 Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. The game checks the purchase receipt on your device and records which items you own in your game account and cloud backup. Google keeps its own record of your purchase history.

1.8 Advertising (after you consent)

The game can show optional ads — a rewarded video you choose to watch for extra coins, and an occasional ad between matches. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with (such as Unity Ads and Google AdMob). Before any ad is loaded, the game asks for your consent through a consent form. If you agree, these services may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and — if you allow it — personalise them. If you decline, you can still play the whole game; you may simply not be offered ads or may see non-personalised ones. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

1.9 Analytics (after you consent)

With your consent, the game sends usage events (such as matches started and finished, screens opened and purchases made) and technical information (device model, operating system, app version) to Unity Analytics, so we can find problems and improve the game. You can withdraw consent at any time under Settings → Privacy choices.

1.10 Connection and technical information

Some information leaves your phone whenever the game uses the network, with or without an account and without a consent choice, because the services cannot work without it:

  • Your IP address and an approximate location. Every request to Unity's services (sections 1.2–1.6) and to Google Play carries your device's IP address, from which the services may work out an approximate location, such as your country or region, to route the request, keep the service secure and measure how it performs. This includes the shop's public list of items and prices, which the game downloads from Unity Cloud Content Delivery without signing you in. We never ask for or use your precise location.
  • Diagnostics. Google Play Billing, the purchasing library that talks to it (section 1.7) and Google Play Games (section 1.3) send technical information such as error reports and performance data, so that purchases and sign-in work and their problems can be fixed.

3. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for other companies' own marketing. We share it only:

  • with other players, as described in sections 1.5 and 1.6;
  • with the service providers that run the game for us, who process it on our behalf:
  • Unity Technologies — Unity Authentication, Cloud Save, Lobby, Relay, Friends, Cloud Content Delivery (the shop's public list of items and prices, downloaded with no account), LevelPlay, Unity Ads and Unity Analytics (Unity privacy policy);
  • Google — Google Play Games, Google Play Billing and AdMob (Google privacy policy);
  • when the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our players or ourselves.

These providers may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, such transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

4. How long we keep it

  • Information on your device stays until you delete the account (section 5), clear the app's data, or uninstall the game.
  • Your game account and cloud data are kept while your account exists, and are deleted when you delete it.
  • Replays: the game keeps your last ten finished matches; older ones are removed automatically.
  • Ad and analytics providers keep information according to their own retention policies.

5. Deleting your account

  • In the game: open Account → Delete account and confirm. This deletes your Unity player account and wipes the game's progress, coins, owned items, replays and friends data from your phone.
  • By email: if you cannot use the game any more, email support@altforgestudio.com from any address with the subject "Delete my Durak: Wildcards account" and your player name and tag or friend code (shown on the Account screen). We will delete your account and its cloud data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. Purchases are tied to your Google Play account; Google keeps its own purchase records, which we cannot delete.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting what happened before). To use any of these rights, email support@altforgestudio.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Information travels between the game and Unity's services over encrypted connections, and online matches are encrypted through Unity Relay. No system is perfectly secure, but we use the protections these services provide and keep access to the game's back-end limited to ourselves.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at the same address. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the game.
